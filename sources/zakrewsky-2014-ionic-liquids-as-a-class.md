## One-line Summary
Supporting Information for the original CAGE discovery paper: detailed synthesis/purification protocols for all 12 ILs/DES tested, including low-temperature recrystallization of geranic acid and physicochemical/toxicity/skin penetration data tables.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: PNAS 111(36): 13313–13318 (2014) — this file is the SI
- **DOI**: 10.1073/pnas.1403995111
- **Type**: Supporting Information (methods, tables, figures)

## 2. Key Contributions
- **Geranic acid purification protocol**: 5–7× recrystallisation from 70 wt% geranic acid / 30 wt% acetone at −70°C
- **CYPHOS 101 (tetraalkylphosphonium chloride) purification**: washed with 1 M NaHCO₃ and water; hexane extraction until UV-Vis > 300 nm disappeared; dried at 80°C under vacuum 24 h
- **Full IL panel**: 12 materials (DES and ILs) systematically compared for biofilm activity and skin penetration
- **Physicochemical data**: viscosity, density, conductivity, Log P, MW for all 12 ILs (Table S3)
- **IC₅₀ data**: cytotoxicity in NHBE cells for all 12 ILs (Table S4)
- **Mechanistic insight**: FTIR confirms ILs act as stratum corneum lipid **extractors** (not fluidizers)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture

### Geranic Acid Purification
- Source: commercial technical grade (Sigma-Aldrich)
- Method: **repeated (5–7×) recrystallisation** from 70 wt% geranic acid / 30 wt% acetone at **−70°C**
- Purity assessment: 1H NMR spectroscopy + conductivity measurements

### CYPHOS 101 Purification
- Source: gift from Cytec Specialty Chemicals
- Wash: 1 M sodium bicarbonate + water; extract with hexanes until UV-Vis absorption > 300 nm disappears and pH stable
- Dry: 80°C under vacuum for 24 h

### Lipophilicity (Log P)
- Shake-flask method: 250 mL n-octanol pre-saturated with 100 mL ddH₂O overnight
- 0.01 M IL solutions; exceptions at lower/higher concentrations due to detector limits

- Geraniol (parent of geranic acid) CPE: enhancement ratio 47.5, irritation potential 53 — highest of all CPEs tested
- Designed CAGE (11) and compound 12 based on geranate anion

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks

### Toxicity — IC₅₀ in NHBE cells (Table S4)
- **Choline geranate (11)**: >>2 mM → essentially non-toxic at tested concentrations
- BZBN (2): 0.01 mM (most toxic)
- Choline oleate (8): 0.03 mM
- Tetraalkylphosphonium geranate (12): 0.39 mM

### Physicochemical Properties (Table S3, compound 11 = CAGE)
- Viscosity: 1345 cP
- Density: 0.990 g/mL
- Conductivity: 0.043 mS/cm
- Molarity: 2.26 M
- Log P: 0.28
- MW: 438.6 g/mol

### Biofilm Activity (Fig. S2)
- S. enterica and P. aeruginosa 24-h biofilms
- Compound 11 (CAGE) among the most effective with low toxicity combination

### Skin Penetration (Figs. S3–S4)
- Mannitol and cefadroxil penetration measured layer-by-layer (tape-stripping: SC1, SC2-5, SC6-10, epidermis, dermis, acceptor)
- CAGE enhanced drug delivery into deep skin layers

### FTIR Mechanism (Fig. S5)
- CH₂ symmetric stretching peak analysis before/after IL application
- Conclusion: ILs extract SC lipids (not merely fluidize them)

## 7. Glossary
- **CAGE**: Compound 11 — choline geranate (2 equiv.), also classified as DES
- **DES**: Deep eutectic solvent
- **CYPHOS 101**: Trihexyltetradecylphosphonium chloride — tetraalkylphosphonium IL
- **CPE**: Chemical penetration enhancer
- **SC**: Stratum corneum — outermost skin barrier
- **NHBE**: Normal human bronchial epithelial cells (toxicity assay)
- **FTIR**: Fourier transform infrared spectroscopy
