## One-line Summary
A comprehensive review of the biogenesis pathways and silencing mechanisms of siRNAs and miRNAs, covering Dicer structure and function, Argonaute proteins, RISC assembly, and the regulatory diversity across organisms.

## 1. Document Information
- **Title**: Origins and Mechanisms of miRNAs and siRNAs

## 2. Key Contributions
- Provides unified mechanistic framework for siRNA and miRNA biogenesis and function
- Describes Dicer domain architecture and catalytic mechanism for dsRNA processing
- Details RISC assembly and the distinct roles of siRISC vs. miRISC
- Covers the divergence of miRNA and siRNA pathways across organisms (C. elegans, Drosophila, Arabidopsis, mammals)
- Discusses regulatory mechanisms and biological consequences of small RNA silencing for chromatin, transcription, and translation
- Historical context from lin-4 discovery (1993) through siRNA (1998) to miRNA class identification (2001)

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Narrative review integrating genetic, biochemical, and structural studies across multiple model organisms
- Three main categories of small RNAs: siRNAs, miRNAs, and piRNAs (piRNAs reviewed separately by Malone/Hannon)
- Dicer mechanism: PAZ domain binds RNA end with ~2 nt 3' overhang; substrate extends two helical turns to RNase III processing center; two RNase III active sites cleave both strands to yield ~21–23 nt duplex with 2 nt 3' overhangs and 5' monophosphates
- Dicer domain organization (N to C): DEXD/H ATPase, DUF283, PAZ, two RNase III domains, dsRBD
- Organisms differ in Dicer specialization: mammals/nematodes have one Dicer; Drosophila has Dcr-1 (miRNA) and Dcr-2 (siRNA); Arabidopsis has four DCL proteins
- Argonaute proteins contain PAZ (sRNA binding) and PIWI (slicer) domains; Ago clade binds si/miRNAs, Piwi clade binds piRNAs
- RISC assembly: Dicer hands off duplex to Argonaute; passenger strand is discarded; guide strand programs RISC specificity

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- miRNA history: lin-4 (C. elegans, 1993) as endogenous developmental regulator; RNAi (1998); 20–25 nt silencing-associated RNAs in plants (1999); siRNA discovery; miRNA as broad class (2001)
- Dicer length ruler: PAZ-to-RNase III distance determines small RNA size (~21–23 nt for most Dicers)
- ATP roles in Dicer: ATP stimulates Drosophila Dcr-2 and C. elegans Dcr-1 processing; dispensable for human Dcr; ATPase domain has autoinhibitory effect on hDcr efficiency
- Mammalian miRNA biogenesis: Drosha/DGCR8 microprocessor generates pre-miRNA in nucleus; Exportin-5 exports pre-miRNA; cytoplasmic Dicer processes to ~22 nt duplex
- siRNA pathway: long dsRNA → Dicer → siRNA duplex → RISC loading → target cleavage (slicing) at position 10/11 from 5' end
- miRNA translational repression mechanisms: deadenylation, decapping, P-body sequestration, inhibition of translation initiation/elongation

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Mechanistic basis for translational inhibition by miRNAs remains incompletely understood
- The distinction between endogenous siRNAs and miRNAs is increasingly blurred
- ATPase domain function in Dicer varies across organisms and remains not fully understood
- Conditions under which autoinhibition of hDcr is relieved are unknown
- Chromatin-level consequences of siRNA pathways in mammals are not well characterized

## 7. Glossary
- **siRNA**: Short interfering RNA; ~21–23 nt RNA derived from long dsRNA; guides RISC to complementary targets for cleavage
- **miRNA**: MicroRNA; ~21–23 nt RNA derived from genomic stem-loop precursors; guides RISC to partially complementary targets
- **piRNA**: Piwi-interacting RNA; ~24–30 nt RNA in germline; binds Piwi-clade Argonautes
- **Dicer**: RNase III family enzyme that processes dsRNA precursors into ~21–23 nt duplexes
- **Argonaute (AGO)**: Core effector protein of RISC; contains PAZ (sRNA binding) and PIWI (slicer) domains
- **RISC**: RNA-induced silencing complex; AGO protein + guide small RNA
- **PAZ domain**: RNA-binding domain found in Dicer and Argonaute; binds 2 nt 3' overhangs of small RNA duplexes
- **PIWI domain**: Structural fold with endonuclease activity in Argonaute; catalyzes target RNA slicing
- **Drosha**: Nuclear RNase III; processes pri-miRNA to pre-miRNA in animals
- **DGCR8**: dsRNA-binding partner of Drosha in the microprocessor complex
- **Exportin-5**: Nuclear export receptor for pre-miRNAs
- **P-bodies**: Cytoplasmic foci containing mRNA decay machinery; sites of miRNA-mediated silencing
- **RNA silencing**: Collective term for small RNA-guided regulatory mechanisms including RNAi, miRNA regulation, and related pathways
