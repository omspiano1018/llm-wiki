## One-line Summary
A 10th-anniversary review of CAGE (choline and geranic acid ionic liquid) covering its discovery, drug delivery applications across multiple routes, and clinical translation — the first drug delivery ionic liquid to enter human clinical trials.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: Journal of Controlled Release, Vol. 376 (2024), pp. 593–600
- **Type**: Review article
- **Published**: 25 October 2024

## 2. Key Contributions
- Comprehensive historical account of CAGE from its 2014 discovery to 2024
- Catalogues all major drug delivery applications: transdermal, oral/buccal, sustained release, antiseptic, tissue ablation, periodontitis, hand hygiene
- Documents CAGE's entry into clinical trials — first drug delivery IL to do so
- Reports successful Phase 2 clinical trial in Atopic Dermatitis patients
- Notes CAGE's inclusion in a commercially launched product

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- CAGE = choline bicarbonate + geranic acid via salt metathesis; stoichiometry variants [Chol]1[Ger]1, [Chol]1[Ger]2, [Chol]1[Ger]4
- Classification: sits at the IL/deep eutectic solvent (DES) boundary depending on stoichiometry and water content
- Transdermal mechanism: penetrates stratum corneum, extracts and fluidises skin lipids; [Chol]1[Ger]2 most potent for permeation
- Macro-molecule delivery: demonstrated up to 150 kDa dextrans and siRNA across skin
- Oral route: CAGE-insulin (10 U/kg) reduced blood glucose 45% in rats; also enhances sorafenib oral absorption

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- >200 patients tested across Phase 1 and Phase 2 studies
- Phase 2 Atopic Dermatitis trial: successful outcome
- Transdermal insulin: first in vivo systemic delivery via CAGE (Banerjee et al.)
- Imiquimod-CAGE: >10-fold greater topical delivery vs. clinical comparator Aldara™
- ILs as a group outperform conventional chemical permeation enhancers in Hansen solubility parameter analysis

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Classification ambiguity (IL vs. DES) complicates regulatory characterisation
- Long-term safety data still accumulating
- Variability with stoichiometry and water content requires careful formulation control

## 6. Related Work
- Zakrewsky et al. 2014 — original CAGE discovery (transdermal small molecules)
- Greene et al. 2019 — CAGE as broad-spectrum topical antiseptic (ESKAPE biofilms)
- Ko et al. 2020 — first clinical translation of CAGE for rosacea
- Kim et al. 2022 — CAGE-based subcutaneous depot for apomorphine in Parkinson's

## 7. Glossary
- **CAGE**: Choline And GEranic acid; biocompatible IL/DES for drug delivery
- **IL**: Ionic liquid — organic salt liquid below 100°C
- **DES**: Deep eutectic solvent — mixture with melting point below either component
- **Stratum corneum**: Outermost skin layer; primary barrier to transdermal delivery
- **API-IL**: Active pharmaceutical ingredient paired as an ionic liquid salt
