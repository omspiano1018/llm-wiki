## One-line Summary
CAGE eradicates biofilms of 11 clinically isolated ESKAPE pathogens including MDR strains at concentrations as low as 3.56 mM in 2 hours, outperforming standard-of-care antiseptics, via a membrane-disrupting mechanism revealed by molecular modelling.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: PLOS ONE 14(9): e0222211
- **Published**: September 17, 2019
- **Type**: Research article

## 2. Key Contributions
- First detailed characterisation of CAGE antibiofilm activity against a panel of 11 ESKAPE pathogens (clinically isolated, including MDR strains)
- Quantified rate of biofilm eradication: 0.1% v:v CAGE achieves >3 log10 reduction in 15 min for S. aureus biofilms
- Demonstrated EPS disruption by CAGE within 120 min
- Proposed mechanism of action via molecular modelling: geranate ion + geranic acid integrate into bacterial membranes → membrane thinning and homeostasis disruption

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- CAGE synthesised by salt metathesis of choline bicarbonate and geranic acid; confirmed by 1H/13C NMR; conductivity 43 mS/cm
- Biofilm assay: MBEC inoculators (Innovotech); 24 h and 72 h old biofilms
- Pathogens: 11 clinically isolated ESKAPE organisms (E. faecium, S. aureus, K. pneumoniae, A. baumanii, P. aeruginosa, Enterobacter sp.)
- Minimum biofilm eradication concentration (MBEC) measured at 3.56 mM (0.156% v:v)
- Molecular dynamics modelling of geranate/geranic acid interaction with bacterial membrane bilayers

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- MBEC: 3.56 mM (0.156% v:v) — eradicates biofilms in 2 h
- 0.1% v:v CAGE: >3 log10 (>1000-fold) reduction in S. aureus biofilm viability within 15 min
- EPS disruption observed at 0.1% v:v within 120 min
- Efficacy maintained against both 24 h and 72 h old biofilms
- Outperforms most FDA-recognised topical antiseptics in potency and speed

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- In vitro biofilm model; in vivo wound efficacy not tested here
- CAGE darkens after >4 months storage at room temperature (potency unaffected in <3 month samples)
- Mechanism of action inferred from modelling; direct structural biology validation needed

## 7. Glossary
- **ESKAPE**: Enterococcus faecium, S. aureus, K. pneumoniae, A. baumanii, P. aeruginosa, Enterobacter sp. — priority nosocomial pathogens
- **MBEC**: Minimum Biofilm Eradication Concentration
- **EPS**: Exopolymeric substances — protective matrix surrounding biofilms
- **MDR**: Multidrug resistant
- **CAGE**: Choline And GEranic acid ionic liquid/deep eutectic solvent
