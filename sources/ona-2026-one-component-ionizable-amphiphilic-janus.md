## One-line Summary
A self-assembling, **one-component ionizable amphiphilic Janus dendrimer (IAJD97)** coassembles with mRNA in a single mixing step to form onion-like dendrimersome nanoparticles that target the **spleen and lymph nodes** (not liver), stay stable at **+4 °C for ≥26 weeks**, and — as a norovirus mRNA vaccine — elicit neutralizing-antibody and T-cell responses **equivalent to the clinical four-component SM102 LNP**, while eliminating cholesterol, phospholipid, and PEG-lipid.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Advances a **one-component delivery system (IAJD)** that replaces the standard **four-component LNP** (ionizable lipid + cholesterol + phospholipid + PEG-lipid) with a **single self-assembling sequence-defined Janus dendrimer** — simplifying synthesis, removing cholesterol/PEG, and improving scalability for pandemic response.
- **IAJD97 lead** selected from screening >300 IAJDs (from a library of 31 "simplified single–single" sSS IAJDs where the ionizable amine is the hydrophilic head): two nitrogen atoms + a hydroxyethyl-capped amino head drive **spleen/lymph-node-selective** mRNA delivery.
- **Cold-chain advantage**: formulation stable at **+4 °C ≥ 26 weeks**; encapsulated mRNA retains **DV200 ≈ 93–95%** after 7 months at 4 °C, vs **28%** for the same mRNA in an SM102 four-component LNP.
- **Vaccine proof of concept (norovirus)**: NW/GI.1 and CapeTown2012/GII.4 VP1 mRNA-IAJD97 elicit neutralizing antibodies, durable to ≥day 150, plus polyfunctional CD4/CD8 T cells and TFH/germinal-center B cells — **comparable to the clinical SM102 LNP**.
- **PEG-free / cholesterol-free** design aimed at avoiding anti-PEG immunity and structural-lipid burden.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **IAJD platform**: ionizable amphiphilic Janus dendrimer — a sequence-defined single molecule with an ionizable amino hydrophilic head and hydrophobic dendritic tails; **coassembles with mRNA by one-step mixing** into "dendrimersome" nanoparticles. Cryo-TEM shows a **multilamellar, onion-like** structure.
- **IAJD97**: naturally-occurring amino heads, two nitrogen atoms, hydroxyethyl top group; easy to synthesize; spleen/LN targeting.
- **mRNA**: nucleoside-modified; reporters Luc (firefly luciferase, short half-life → readout of ongoing translation) and norovirus VP1 antigens (Norwalk/GI.1, CapeTown2012/GII.4).
- **Formulation buffers**: acetate (pH ~4.81) or PBS-diluted (pH ~6.02); doses 5–10 µg mRNA/mouse (toxicology up to 30 µg).
- **Characterization**: particle diameter, PDI, encapsulation efficiency, **pKa**, zeta potential; cryo-TEM; RNA integrity by capillary gel electrophoresis (Agilent Bioanalyzer, DV200).
- **In vivo (mouse, BALB/c)**: IVIS bioluminescence biodistribution/kinetics; IV and i.m. immunization (days 0/28); neutralizing-Ab surrogate (HBGA/VLP blockade, ID50); intracellular-cytokine flow cytometry for CD4/CD8 T cells; TFH and GC B-cell enumeration; histopathology (H&E) and serum ALT/AST for toxicity.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- **Biodistribution**: spleen protein expression **590× heart, 18× lung, 9× liver** — strongly lymphoid-organ-selective (contrasts the liver tropism of conventional LNPs driven by ApoE/LDL-R).
- **Kinetics**: whole-body luciferase ~45% of initial at 24 h; spleen signal detectable to **96 h**.
- **Stability**: stable at +4 °C through 26 weeks (acetate); PBS formulation stable ~20 weeks; mRNA DV200 93.2% (GII.4) / 94.7% (Luc) after 7 months vs SM102 LNP 28% — far exceeds the COVID mRNA vaccines' 5–30 day 2–8 °C shelf lives.
- **Toxicity**: no inflammation/structural change in heart, lung, liver, spleen, kidney at 10–30 µg; only **mild–moderate, likely reversible PALS lymphocyte depletion** in spleen at 30 µg; no ALT/AST elevation.
- **Humoral immunity**: high nAb titers across 1–10 µg doses (minimal effective dose <1 µg); **comparable to SM102** at day 56; durable to **day 150** (ID50 ~3000 at 5 µg i.m.).
- **Cellular immunity**: antigen-specific polyfunctional CD4/CD8 T cells (IFN-γ, IL-2, TNF-α; Th1-biased), triple-positive CD4 T cells, robust TFH + GC B-cell induction from a single dose — equivalent adjuvant activity to LNPs (attributed to the ionizable component).

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- **Mouse-only**, single pathogen (norovirus) proof of concept; primarily IV dosing (some i.m.); effective dose (5–10 µg) higher than the 1–3 µg typical for mouse LNP vaccinations.
- **Mechanism of spleen/LN targeting not fully resolved** — hypothesized net-negative protein-corona/surface-hydration effects, not directly demonstrated.
- 40-week stability and the sharp post-40-week decline based on **n = 1** (no statistics).
- Late-timepoint circulation, organ-specific antigen expression, and potential localized immune effects of VP1 not assessed; long-term safety and large-animal/clinical translation pending.

## 6. Related Work
- **Four-component LNPs** (Comirnaty®, Spikevax®; SM102 / DLin-MC3-DMA): the benchmark the IAJD platform simplifies and is compared against head-to-head (immunogenicity, RNA-integrity stability).
- **Prior IAJD/dendrimersome work** (Percec, Weissman; refs 15–18, 25, 26): sequence-defined Janus dendrimers; the 31-member sSS IAJD library from which IAJD97 derives.
- **Hare et al. (ref 27)** tiered mRNA-vaccine storage framework — the cold-chain context for the 26-week 4 °C claim.
- Within this wiki: conceptually parallels the **one-component / lipid-is-the-carrier** simplification of LNPs and connects to the **thermostability / cold-chain-free storage** theme.

## 7. Glossary
- **IAJD (Ionizable Amphiphilic Janus Dendrimer)**: a single sequence-defined amphiphile (two-faced "Janus" architecture) with an ionizable amino head; self-assembles with mRNA into dendrimersome nanoparticles — a one-component alternative to the four-component LNP.
- **Dendrimersome**: vesicle/onion-like multilamellar nanoparticle self-assembled from Janus dendrimers.
- **sSS IAJD (simplified single–single)**: minimal IAJD subclass where the ionizable amine itself is the hydrophilic domain; IAJD97 is a member.
- **Four-component LNP**: ionizable lipid + cholesterol + phospholipid (helper) + PEG-lipid — the standard mRNA-vaccine carrier.
- **DV200**: % of RNA fragments >200 nt; an integrity/quality metric (higher = more intact).
- **pKa**: acid dissociation constant of the ionizable amine; tunes endosomal protonation and delivery.
- **HBGA blockade / VLP neutralization**: surrogate neutralizing-antibody assay for norovirus (no culture system).
- **TFH / GC B cells**: T follicular helper and germinal-center B cells — drivers of durable, high-affinity antibody responses.
