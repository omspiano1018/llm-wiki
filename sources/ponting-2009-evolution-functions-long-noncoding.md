## One-line Summary
A review of the evolutionary signatures and functional roles of long noncoding RNAs (lncRNAs), discussing pervasive transcription, the evidence for lncRNA functionality in transcriptional and epigenetic regulation, and their potential roles in disease.

## 1. Document Information
- **Title**: Evolution and Functions of Long Noncoding RNAs
- **Authors**: Chris P. Ponting (MRC Functional Genomics Unit, University of Oxford), Peter L. Oliver (University of Oxford), Wolf Reik (The Babraham Institute; University of Cambridge)

- **Journal**: Cell, Vol. 136, pp. 629–641
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.cell.2009.02.006
- **Type**: Review article (Cell RNA special issue)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Defines lncRNAs (>200 nt, non-protein-coding) and places them in the context of pervasive eukaryotic transcription
- Reviews evidence for lncRNA functionality using evolutionary conservation signatures vs. transcriptional noise debate
- Catalogs lncRNA categories: intronic/antisense/intergenic/bidirectional and sense/antisense orientations
- Discusses well-established lncRNA functions in epigenetic gene regulation: Xist (X chromosome inactivation), HOTAIR (Polycomb targeting), H19/Igf2 imprinting locus
- Examines lncRNA roles in transcriptional regulation via enhancer RNAs, gene looping, and interference with TF binding
- Reviews lncRNA roles in disease including cancer, neurological disorders, and imprinting diseases

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Narrative review combining evolutionary analysis (sequence conservation, evolutionary rate), transcriptome data (tiling arrays, RNA-seq, CAGE tags), and experimental functional studies
- Pervasive transcription data: ~180,000 mouse cDNAs from ~44,000 transcript clusters; ~20,210 protein-coding loci; noncoding transcript clusters outnumber coding clusters; only ~1% of human genome encodes protein, ~4–9% transcribed but non-coding
- CAGE (cap analysis of gene expression): identifies transcription start sites genome-wide including non-canonical locations (within exons, near 3' ends of UTRs)
- lncRNA categories: (1) intronic sense or antisense; (2) antisense overlapping coding genes; (3) intergenic (lincRNAs); (4) bidirectional; (5) pseudogenes
- Evolutionary analysis: lncRNA sequences evolve faster than protein-coding sequences but slower than non-transcribed intergenic sequence, suggesting weak purifying selection on some lncRNAs
- Imprinting as a model: H19/Igf2 locus; CTCF-dependent chromatin insulator model; Igf2r/Air antisense lncRNA model for paternal allele silencing via Polycomb recruitment

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Approximately 180,000 mouse cDNAs identified; noncoding transcript clusters more numerous than coding
- ~30% of noncoding RNAs show at least one alternative transcript
- ~50% of human/mouse genes exhibit alternative polyadenylation
- Xist lncRNA (~17 kb) coats the inactive X chromosome and recruits Polycomb repressive complex 2 (PRC2) via Ezh2 interaction; RepA domain within Xist required for PRC2 recruitment
- HOTAIR: antisense lncRNA from HOXC locus; silences HOXD genes in trans by recruiting PRC2; overexpressed in breast cancer and correlates with metastasis
- H19: paternally imprinted, maternally expressed lncRNA; shares regulatory elements with Igf2; CTCF insulator blocks enhancer access on maternal allele
- Air: antisense lncRNA at Igf2r locus; required for paternal imprinting of Igf2r, Slc22a2, Slc22a3; accumulates at Kcnq1ot1-like nuclear domains; recruits G9a histone methyltransferase
- Evf2: lncRNA from Dlx5/6 locus; activates Dlx5/6 transcription by recruiting DLX2 transcription factor; expressed in developing brain
- Many lncRNAs expressed at low levels and in tissue/developmental stage-restricted patterns

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- The vast majority of proposed lncRNAs lack experimental functional validation
- It remains unclear how much pervasive transcription is biologically functional vs. transcriptional noise
- Evolutionary conservation alone is insufficient to prove functionality, as some functional lncRNAs are poorly conserved
- Mechanistic understanding of how most lncRNAs achieve their effects is lacking
- Disease associations are largely correlative; causal mechanisms for most disease-associated lncRNAs are unknown

## 7. Glossary
- **lncRNA**: Long noncoding RNA; transcripts >200 nt with no protein-coding capacity
- **Pervasive transcription**: The observation that essentially the entire eukaryotic genome is transcribed at some point in some cell type
- **CAGE**: Cap analysis of gene expression; maps 5' ends of capped transcripts to identify transcription start sites
- **Xist**: X-inactive specific transcript; lncRNA that coats the inactive X chromosome and recruits Polycomb repressive machinery
- **HOTAIR**: HOX antisense intergenic RNA; lncRNA that recruits PRC2 in trans to silence HOXD genes
- **PRC2**: Polycomb repressive complex 2; histone H3K27 methyltransferase recruited by some lncRNAs to silence target genes
- **H19**: Imprinted lncRNA at the H19/Igf2 locus; maternally expressed; regulates Igf2 imprinting via CTCF insulator
- **Air**: Antisense Igf2r RNA; lncRNA required for paternal imprinting of Igf2r cluster; recruits G9a methyltransferase
- **CTCF**: CCCTC-binding factor; zinc-finger protein that mediates chromatin insulation at imprinting control regions
- **Imprinting**: Epigenetic mechanism by which genes are expressed from only one parental allele
- **lincRNA**: Long intergenic noncoding RNA; lncRNA located between protein-coding genes
- **TUF**: Transcript of unknown function; alternative term sometimes used for lncRNAs
- **Evf2**: Noncoding RNA from Dlx5/6 locus; activates Dlx gene transcription by recruiting DLX2 protein
