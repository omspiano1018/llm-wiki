## One-line Summary
A comprehensive review of the origin, biogenesis pathway, and modes of action of plant microRNAs, highlighting both shared features with and key differences from metazoan miRNA biology.

## 1. Document Information
- **Title**: Origin, Biogenesis, and Activity of Plant MicroRNAs
- **Authors**: Olivier Voinnet (Institut de Biologie Moleculaire des Plantes, CNRS UPR2357–Universite de Strasbourg)
- **Year**: 2009
- **Journal**: Cell, Vol. 136, pp. 669–687
- **DOI**: 10.1016/j.cell.2009.01.046
- **Type**: Review article (part of Cell's RNA special issue)

## 2. Key Contributions
- Provides a unified framework for understanding the diversity of small RNA (sRNA) species in plants, including miRNAs, siRNAs, and trans-acting siRNAs
- Describes the evolutionary origin of plant MIR genes via inverted duplication events and spontaneous evolution from random inverted repeats
- Details the plant-specific biogenesis machinery: DDL, SERRATE (SE), HYL1, DCL1, CBC, HASTY, and HEN1
- Distinguishes plant miRNA action (predominantly slicing due to near-perfect complementarity) from metazoan translational inhibition
- Covers regulatory layers controlling miRNA processing and activity, including methylation by HEN1 and degradation by SDN nucleases
- Discusses the blurring boundary between miRNA and siRNA classes in plants

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Narrative review synthesizing genetic and biochemical studies, primarily in Arabidopsis thaliana
- Covers four consensus RNA-silencing steps: dsRNA induction, dsRNA processing into 18–25 nt sRNAs, 3'-O-methylation, and RISC incorporation
- Describes the plant sRNA multitude: dominated by 24 nt heterochromatin-associated siRNAs processed by DCL3 and loaded into AGO4/AGO6 for RdDM; miRNAs are the second most abundant class
- Biogenesis pathway: Pol II transcription of pri-miRNA → stabilization by DDL → processing in nuclear D-bodies by DCL1/HYL1/SE/CBC → pre-miRNA export via HASTY → cytoplasmic methylation by HEN1 → AGO1 loading
- Plant MIR gene evolution modeled as a two-phase process: inverted duplication generating a proto-MIR (processed as siRNAs by DCL3/DCL4), followed by selective refinement to produce a single miRNA processed by DCL1
- Target regulation: predominantly slicing (AGO1 slicer activity); translational inhibition also documented for some miRNAs

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Plant miRNAs are 20–24 nt and use near-perfect complementarity to single target sites, mostly in coding sequences of transcription factors and growth regulators
- Metazoan miRNAs use imperfect complementarity to multiple 3' UTR sites, typically causing translational repression
- DCL1 produces 18–21 nt sRNAs; DCL2, DCL3, DCL4 produce 22, 24, 21 nt products respectively
- HEN1 methylation protects miRNAs from SDN-class exonuclease degradation
- Arabidopsis has 10 AGO proteins (AGO1–10); AGO1 and AGO4 have demonstrated slicer activity
- Young MIR genes can evolve from inverted duplications; ancient MIR genes show conserved stem-loop structure compatible with DCL1 processing
- miRNA-deficient mutants (e.g., dcl1, ago1) show severe developmental defects in both plants and animals

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- The distinction between miRNA and siRNA is becoming blurred in plants; functional categories may overlap
- Mechanisms of translational inhibition by plant miRNAs are incompletely understood
- The full extent of miRNA targets (beyond transcription factors) remains to be characterized
- Export of pre-miRNAs by HASTY and other factors is not fully characterized
- Regulatory mechanisms controlling HYL1 and SE activity in response to environmental signals need elucidation

## 7. Glossary
- **miRNA**: MicroRNA; ~21 nt RNA processed from stem-loop precursors, guides RISC to complementary targets
- **siRNA**: Short interfering RNA; processed from long dsRNA, primarily targets transposons and foreign nucleic acids
- **DCL1–4**: Dicer-like proteins 1–4 in Arabidopsis; produce sRNAs of different sizes
- **AGO1**: ARGONAUTE1; main effector protein for miRNA-directed slicing in plants
- **HEN1**: Methyltransferase that adds 2'-O-methyl group to sRNA 3' ends, protecting from degradation
- **HYL1**: HYPONASTIC LEAVES1; dsRNA-binding protein involved in DCL1-mediated miRNA processing
- **SERRATE (SE)**: C2H2 zinc finger protein; cofactor of DCL1 processing complex
- **HASTY**: Plant exportin-5 ortholog; involved in pre-miRNA nuclear export
- **RdDM**: RNA-directed DNA methylation; epigenetic silencing pathway using 24 nt siRNAs
- **RISC**: RNA-induced silencing complex; effector complex containing AGO protein and guide sRNA
- **D-body**: Nuclear processing center where pri-miRNA to pre-miRNA conversion occurs
- **DDL**: DAWDLE; RNA-binding protein that stabilizes pri-miRNAs
- **SDN**: SMALL RNA DEGRADING NUCLEASE; exonucleases that degrade unmethylated sRNAs
- **TGS**: Transcriptional gene silencing; silencing mediated by chromatin-level mechanisms
- **PTGS**: Post-transcriptional gene silencing; silencing at the mRNA level
