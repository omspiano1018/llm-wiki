## One-line Summary
Focused 2024 review (Int. J. Biological Macromolecules) on ionic liquids as biocompatible, tunable carriers for nucleic-acid (DNA, siRNA, mRNA, miRNA, ASO) delivery, emphasizing how ILs improve solubility/stability of nucleic acids, enhance cellular uptake, and serve as components of nano delivery vectors (liposomes, nanoparticles) to boost transfection.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Delivery-centric (vs. handling/storage) review dedicated to **nucleic acid therapeutics**: DNA, siRNA, mRNA, miRNA, ASO
- Frames the core delivery problem: nucleic acids are large, strongly anionic, nuclease-sensitive, and poorly membrane-permeant → require vectors
- Surveys ILs in two roles: (i) **direct solubilizers/stabilizers** of nucleic acids in aqueous media and uptake enhancers; (ii) **components of nano-vectors** (liposomes, nanoparticles) that raise pDNA/siRNA transfection efficiency
- Emphasizes biocompatible (choline-, amino-acid-derived) ILs and green-chemistry framing
- Argues IL-based nucleic-acid transport is a crucial emerging therapeutic modality for the coming decades

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
Narrative review. Covers: IL fundamentals (composition, tunability, green-solvent history incl. BASIL); IL roles in API/biopolymer solubilization (hydrotropy, cosolvency, micellization); the delivery challenges of nucleic acid classes (DNA, siRNA, mRNA, miRNA, ASO) and their secondary structures (duplex, triplex, G-quadruplex); IL strategies for extraction/concentration/stabilization/intracellular spreading; and IL integration into nano delivery systems with reported transfection outcomes.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Documents IL-driven aqueous **solubility enhancement** for many poorly soluble APIs (amphotericin B, albendazole, danazol, itraconazole, 5-fluorouracil, paclitaxel, etc.) as evidence of IL solvation power transferable to formulation
- Catalogs API-ILs across pharmacological classes (e.g., ranitidinium docusate, procainium salicylate, lidocainium ibuprofenate) with retained bioactivity
- Reviews IL formulations reported to **preserve native DNA/RNA structure**, protect against degradation, and **enhance cellular uptake / transfection** of plasmid DNA and siRNA via IL-containing liposomes and nanoparticles
- Highlights successful **RNA delivery** to target sites enabled by ILs as the review's headline finding

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Toxicity and long-term safety of many ILs remain the principal barrier; biocompatible choline/amino-acid ILs are the favored path
- Need for standardized in vivo efficacy/safety data and scalable, regulatory-acceptable formulations
- Positions IL nucleic-acid delivery as nascent but high-potential; calls for rational ion design and integration with established nano-delivery platforms

## 7. Glossary
- **ASO (antisense oligonucleotide)**: short single-stranded oligo complementary to a target RNA
- **Hydrotropy**: solubilization of a poorly soluble solute by a co-solute that disrupts water structure / self-aggregates
- **API-IL**: ionic liquid whose ion(s) is an active pharmaceutical ingredient
- **Transfection**: introduction of nucleic acid into eukaryotic cells (non-viral here)
- **BASIL**: first commercialized IL-based industrial process (BASF, biphasic acid scavenging utilizing ILs)
- **Nano-vector**: nanoparticle/liposome carrier system for nucleic acids
