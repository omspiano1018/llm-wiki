## One-line Summary
SEAPORT — a CAGE1:2/NMP/water ternary system — self-emulsifies upon subcutaneous injection to entrap apomorphine, extending pharmacokinetics from 3 injections/day to ~3 injections/week in rat and pig models of Parkinson's disease.

## 1. Document Information
- **Journal**: PNAS 2022 Vol. 119 No. 9 e2110450119
- **Published**: February 23, 2022
- **Type**: Research article

## 2. Key Contributions
- Introduces SEAPORT (Self-Emulsifying APO-Releasing Therapeutic) — first CAGE-based sustained-release subcutaneous depot
- Converts apomorphine (APO) from 3×/day to every-other-day dosing in pigs
- Exploits differential miscibility of CAGE1:2 with water and NMP to achieve in situ self-emulsification as the release mechanism
- Solubilises APO at 30 mg/mL (3× higher than Apokyn) while maintaining stability and oxidation resistance

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- Formulation: CAGE1:2 (10% v/v) + NMP (42.7% v/v) + water (47.3% v/v) + APO (30 mg/mL) + SMB antioxidant + PEG 3350 stabiliser
- Mechanism: upon subcutaneous injection NMP diffuses away → CAGE1:2 self-emulsifies in water → APO entrapped in CAGE droplets → sustained release
- Characterisation: ternary phase diagram, LC-MS for APO concentration, stability at 4/25/37°C over 7 days
- In vivo: pharmacokinetics in rats and pigs; compared to Apokyn (clinical comparator)
- Pharmacokinetic simulation used to project human dosing regimen

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- APO solubility in SEAPORT: 30 mg/mL (vs. 10 mg/mL in Apokyn)
- Stability: >90% APO retained over 7 days at 4, 25, 37°C
- Rats: significantly extended APO plasma concentration vs. Apokyn
- Pigs: 48-h detectable drug concentration (vs. ~2 h for Apokyn)
- Projected human regimen: 3 injections/week vs. current 3 injections/day
- All non-CAGE excipients already listed in FDA-approved subcutaneous products

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Animal models (rat, pig) — human PK yet to be confirmed
- NMP is an organic solvent; safety profile at used concentration relies on FDA precedent from ELIGARD
- CAGE1:2 regulatory classification (IL vs. DES) may require novel characterisation methods for IND/NDA

## 7. Glossary
- **SEAPORT**: Self-Emulsifying APO-Releasing Therapeutic
- **APO**: Apomorphine — dopamine agonist for Parkinson's off-episode relief
- **NMP**: N-methyl-2-pyrrolidone — organic cosolvent; FDA-listed in ELIGARD
- **SMB**: Sodium metabisulphite — antioxidant preventing APO oxidation
- **PEG 3350**: Polyethylene glycol; stabiliser
- **MEC/MTC**: Minimum effective concentration (4 ng/mL) / Maximum tolerated concentration (10 ng/mL) for APO in humans
- **Apokyn**: Commercially approved subcutaneous APO formulation (3×/day)
