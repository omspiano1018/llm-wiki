## One-line Summary
A detailed review of spliceosome architecture and function, explaining how its exceptional compositional and structural dynamics enable accurate pre-mRNA splicing and flexible alternative splice-site choice—in contrast to the stable, preformed ribosome.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Contrasts two design principles of RNP machines: the stable, preformed-active-site ribosome versus the highly dynamic, compositionally variable spliceosome.
- Explains the stepwise assembly of the spliceosome (E, A, B, Bact, B*, C complexes) from five snRNPs (U1, U2, U4/U6, U5) and numerous non-snRNP proteins.
- Describes how eight conserved DExH/D-box RNA helicases remodel RNA-RNA and RNA-protein contacts at each assembly transition, driving the cycle forward and providing kinetic proofreading.
- Details the two-step transesterification mechanism of splicing and how the catalytic RNA is composed primarily of U6 snRNA (analogous to group II self-splicing introns).
- Discusses structural data on individual snRNPs and spliceosomal subcomplexes that are building up toward a full understanding of this ~5 MDa machine.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **snRNP structure**: Each snRNP contains one (or two) snRNAs plus a common Sm protein ring (seven Sm proteins: B/B', D1, D2, D3, E, F, G) plus snRNA-specific proteins. U6 and U6atac use Lsm proteins instead of Sm.
- **Assembly pathway**:
  1. E complex: U1 snRNP recognizes 5' splice site; SF1/BBP binds branch point; U2AF binds poly-pyrimidine tract and 3' AG.
  2. A complex: U2 snRNA base-pairs with branch point; ATP-dependent Prp5 and Prp11 helicases assist.
  3. B complex: Tri-snRNP (U4/U6.U5) joins.
  4. Bact: U1 and U4 snRNPs are destabilized and ejected; Prp28 and Brr2 helicases remodel. U6 rearranges to contact 5' splice site.
  5. B*: Prp2 helicase activates catalysis.
  6. C complex: First transesterification (5' splice site attacked by 2'-OH of branch point adenosine, forming lariat intermediate); Prp16 remodels for second step.
  7. Second transesterification: 3'-OH of exon 1 attacks 3' splice site; exons joined; intron lariat released.
  8. Disassembly: Prp22 releases mRNA; Prp43 disassembles lariat-snRNP; components recycled.
- **Catalytic center**: Composed primarily of U6 snRNA (ACAGAGA box, ISL); two Mg2+ ions catalyze transesterification; analogous to group II intron active site.
- **Proofreading**: DExH/D helicases provide kinetic discrimination; sub-optimal splice sites are kinetically rejected at multiple ATP-dependent steps.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
- Structural data by cryo-EM and X-ray crystallography on Sm core, U1 snRNP, and U4/U6 di-snRNP provided atomic-resolution views of individual snRNP components.
- Tri-snRNP (U4/U6.U5) is the largest pre-formed spliceosomal building block: ~1 MDa; its structure was partially solved by cryo-EM.
- The spliceosome contains >100 proteins in humans; many are recruited transiently at specific assembly steps.
- Comparison between spliceosome and group II introns: both use two-metal-ion transesterification, form 2'-5' lariat intermediates, and use analogous RNA elements—supporting the hypothesis that the spliceosome evolved from a group II intron.
- DExH/D helicases (Prp28, Brr2, Prp2, Prp16, Prp22, Prp43, Prp5, Prp11) collectively perform ~8 remodeling events; each driven by ATP hydrolysis.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- No high-resolution structure of the complete spliceosome at any assembly stage was available in 2009.
- The precise molecular mechanism by which helicases achieve specificity (why each helicase acts at a specific step rather than randomly) was not fully understood.
- How splicing is coordinated with transcription (cotranscriptional splicing) and how this influences alternative splicing decisions remained active research areas.
- The structure of the active site during catalysis—the exact positions of the two Mg2+ ions and the reacting groups—was not yet defined.

## 7. Glossary
- **snRNP**: Small nuclear ribonucleoprotein; U1, U2, U4/U6, U5 snRNPs are core spliceosome building blocks.
- **Sm proteins**: Seven-membered ring of proteins (B/B', D1, D2, D3, E, F, G) shared by U1, U2, U4, U5 snRNPs.
- **Lsm proteins**: Sm-like proteins that form the U6 snRNP Sm-like ring.
- **DExH/D-box helicases**: ATP-dependent RNA helicases (D-E-x-D/H motif) that remodel RNA-protein contacts during spliceosome assembly/disassembly.
- **Branch point**: Conserved adenosine in the intron whose 2'-OH attacks the 5' splice site in the first transesterification.
- **Lariat**: Branched intron intermediate formed when the 2'-OH of the branch point attacks the 5' splice site; the intron forms a loop.
- **Tri-snRNP**: Pre-assembled U4/U6.U5 triple-snRNP complex; joins the spliceosome at the B complex stage.
- **Catalytic activation**: The transition from Bact to B* complex; Prp2 helicase remodels the spliceosome to enable first transesterification.
- **E complex**: Earliest recognizable spliceosomal assembly on pre-mRNA; contains U1 snRNP at 5' splice site and U2AF at 3' splice site.
- **ISL**: Internal stem-loop of U6 snRNA; key structural element of the catalytic center.
- **Proofreading (splicing)**: ATP-dependent kinetic discrimination at multiple helicase-driven steps to reject sub-optimal splice sites.
- **Two-step transesterification**: Two sequential SN2-like reactions: (1) branch point 2'-OH attacks 5' splice site; (2) 3'-OH of released exon attacks 3' splice site.
