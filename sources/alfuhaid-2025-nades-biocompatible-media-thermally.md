## One-line Summary
Natural deep eutectic solvents (NADES) based on choline chloride preserve mRNA translatability for at least four months at room temperature and 48 hours at 50°C, with >98% cell viability, enabling cold-chain-free RNA storage.

## 1. Document Information
- **Type**: Preprint (bioRxiv); not peer-reviewed at time of archiving
- **Institution**: King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), Saudi Arabia; Leiden University, Netherlands
- **Download date**: 2026-05-22

## 2. Key Contributions
1. First demonstration that NADES can preserve mRNA integrity and in cellulo translatability at ambient and elevated temperatures without chemical modification of the RNA.
2. Choline chloride-based NADES (A, C, D, E) outperform betaine-based NADES (1–5); NADES C was the best performer.
3. >50% mRNA translatability retained for ≥4 months at 21°C and 48 h at 50°C vs. −80°C control.
4. RNA stability maintained through repeated temperature fluctuations (50°C ↔ 4°C cycles).
5. Selected NADES concentrations maintain ~99% HeLa cell viability, confirming biocompatibility.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
- **NADES preparation**: Betaine (NADES 1–5) and choline chloride (NADES A–E) combined with glycerol and/or disaccharides via heating method (Dai et al. 2013). Only non-crystallizing formulations used.
- **Model RNA**: mCherry mRNA synthesized by in vitro transcription; pseudouridine and 5-methylcytidine modifications included (HighYield T7 ARCA Kit, Jena Biosciences).
- **Storage conditions tested**: 4°C, 21°C (RT), 37°C, 50°C; also temperature-fluctuation cycling (12 h at 50°C / 12 h at 4°C × 2).
- **Translatability assay**: HeLa cells transfected with stored mRNA (Lipofectamine MessengerMAX); % mCherry-positive cells quantified by flow cytometry (Texas Red filter), normalized to −80°C control.
- **Biocompatibility assay**: HeLa cell viability after NADES treatment (2–20 µg/mL), assessed by fluorescence microscopy and flow cytometry.

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
| Condition | Water | NADES C |
|---|---|---|
| 24 h at 37°C | ~85% translatability | ~90% |
| 48 h at 37°C | ~85% | ~85% |
| 24 h at 50°C | ~50% | ~70% |
| 48 h at 50°C | ~15% | ~50% |
| 4 months at 21°C | — | >50% |

- Temperature fluctuation (50°C ↔ 4°C, 4 cycles): NADES-stored mRNA maintained stability; water-stored mRNA degraded progressively.
- Cell viability with NADES: >98% across all tested formulations at selected concentrations.

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- Preprint status: results not yet peer-reviewed.
- Only mCherry mRNA tested as model — generalizability to longer or structurally complex mRNA (e.g., full vaccine constructs in LNPs) not established.
- Betaine-based NADES failed; mechanism of choline chloride superiority not fully explained.
- NADES C composition not explicitly detailed in extracted text (proprietary formulation screening).
- In vivo biocompatibility not assessed (only HeLa cell culture).
- Long-term stability endpoint at RT is 4 months — comparison with leading alternatives (lyophilization, ionic liquids) not included.

## 6. Related Work
- Choline chloride-based DES previously shown to stabilize DNA for 6 months at RT.
- NADES stabilization mechanism attributed to hydrogen bond network reducing free energy and low water activity.
- Complements body of work on alternative RNA storage: ionic liquids (verissimo-2026, yu-2026), lyophilization (khan-2025, zhen-2026), chemical matrices (lou-2014, fabre-2014).
- Pedro-2018 establishes cholinium-based buffers (choline chloride) for biological applications — directly relevant to mechanism.
- Kim-2022 demonstrated deep eutectic solvents for RNA preservation; NADES are a natural-metabolite subset of DES.

## 7. Glossary
- **NADES**: Natural Deep Eutectic Solvents — DES made from natural metabolites (sugars, amino acids, organic acids); subset of DES with enhanced biocompatibility.
- **DES**: Deep Eutectic Solvents — hydrogen-bonded mixtures with melting points below those of individual components; green solvents.
- **In cellulo translatability**: ability of stored mRNA to produce functional protein when transfected into living cells.
- **mCherry**: red fluorescent protein used as reporter; excitation 540–590 nm, emission 550–650 nm.
- **Pseudouridine (Ψ-U)**: modified nucleoside substituted for uridine to reduce mRNA immunogenicity and increase stability.
- **In vitro transcription (IVT)**: cell-free enzymatic synthesis of RNA from a DNA template.
