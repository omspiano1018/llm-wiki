## One-line Summary
A visual reference summarizing the four coupled nuclear steps of mRNP biogenesis—5′ capping, splicing, 3′ end processing, and nuclear export—in yeast and vertebrates, with key RNA elements and protein components.

## 2. Key Contributions
- Provides a compact reference for mRNP assembly covering: (1) 5′ capping, (2) pre-mRNA splicing, (3) 3′ end processing and polyadenylation, (4) nuclear export.
- Highlights that these steps are physically and temporally coupled through RNA Pol II CTD phosphorylation states.
- Notes key differences between yeast and vertebrate systems (intron length, consensus sequences, alternative splicing).
- Points out that >90% of human genes are alternatively spliced and >50% have alternative 3′ ends.

## 3. Methodology and Architecture
**5′ Capping**: 7-methylguanosine cap added by three enzymes (RNA 5′ triphosphatase, guanyltransferase, methyltransferase) recruited via Ser5-phosphorylated RNA Pol II CTD. Cap added at ~20 nt of transcript.

**Splicing**: Catalyzed by spliceosome (5 snRNAs + 80–200 proteins). Sequential assembly: U1 snRNP → commitment complex → U2 snRNP → U4/U6/U5 tri-snRNP. Eight DExH/D-box helicases drive rearrangements. Vertebrate exon junctions marked by EJC (triggers NMD when downstream of stop codon).

**3′ End Processing**: Large cleavage/polyadenylation complex (>20 proteins in yeast, >14 in vertebrates). Poly(A) tail length: 70–90 nt in yeast, 200–300 nt in vertebrates. Pcf11 recruits 3′ processing factors via Ser2-phosphorylated CTD.

**Nuclear Export**: TREX complex links transcription to export. Dimeric export receptor Mex67-Mtr2 (yeast) / TAP-p15/NXF1-NXT1 (vertebrates) shuttles mRNP through FG-repeat nucleoporins. mRNP remodeled on cytoplasmic face by Dbp5 helicase (activated by Gle1 + inositol phosphate).

## 4. Key Results and Benchmarks
Key RNA consensus elements documented:
- Yeast 5′ splice site: /GUAUGU; vertebrate: /GURAG
- Yeast branchpoint: UACUAAC; vertebrate: YURAY
- Vertebrate CPS: AAUAAA; cleavage site: CA; DSE: YGUGUUYY
- All steps coupled through RNA Pol II CTD heptad repeats (YSTPSPS × 26 in yeast, × 52 in vertebrates)

## 5. Limitations and Future Work
- SnapShot format limits mechanistic detail; directed to accompanying literature.
- Regulatory factors (SR proteins, hnRNPs) that mediate alternative splicing not shown in full.
- Quality control by nuclear exosome mentioned but not diagrammed.

## 7. Glossary
- **mRNP**: messenger ribonucleoprotein; mRNA + associated proteins.
- **CTD**: carboxy-terminal domain of RNA Pol II largest subunit; scaffold for processing machinery.
- **EJC**: exon junction complex; marks exon-exon boundaries; triggers NMD if downstream of stop codon.
- **TREX**: transcription-export complex; links transcription to mRNA export.
- **NMD**: nonsense-mediated mRNA decay; degrades mRNAs with premature stop codons.
- **Dbp5**: DEAD-box helicase; remodels mRNP at the cytoplasmic face of the nuclear pore.
- **snRNP**: small nuclear ribonucleoprotein; core spliceosomal components (U1, U2, U4, U5, U6).
