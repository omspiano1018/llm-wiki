## Summary
A Cell SnapShot (visual reference) from Bergkessel, Wilmes, and Guthrie (UCSF) summarizing the four coupled steps of nuclear mRNP biogenesis: **5′ capping → splicing → 3′ end processing → nuclear export**. All steps are coordinated through the phosphorylation state of the RNA Pol II CTD heptad repeat (YSTPSPS). Key differences between yeast and vertebrate systems are noted. Over 90% of human genes are alternatively spliced; over 50% use alternative 3′ ends.

## Key Contributions
- Concise reference for RNA Pol II CTD as the scaffold linking all mRNA processing steps.
- Documents consensus RNA elements for splice sites, branchpoints, and polyadenylation signals in both yeast and vertebrates.
- Highlights EJC deposition at exon junctions as a mechanism coupling splicing to NMD surveillance.
- Shows that Dbp5 helicase remodels the mRNP on the cytoplasmic face of the nuclear pore to prevent re-import.

## Methodology and Architecture
**5′ Capping** (Ser5-phospho CTD):
- 7-methylguanosine cap added at ~20 nt by three enzymes (triphosphatase, guanyltransferase, methyltransferase).
- Nuclear CBC (Cbp80-20) replaced by cytoplasmic eIF4E after export.

**Splicing** (Ser2-phospho CTD):
- Sequential snRNP assembly; eight DExH/D-box helicases drive conformational changes.
- EJC deposited upstream of exon-exon junctions → triggers NMD if downstream of premature stop.

**3′ End Processing** (Ser2-phospho CTD):
- Poly(A) tail: 70–90 nt (yeast) / 200–300 nt (vertebrates).
- Coupled to transcription termination via Pcf11–CTD interaction.

**Nuclear Export**:
- TREX complex bridges transcription and export (TREX2 recruits genes to nuclear pore via SAGA).
- Mex67/TAP-p15/NXF1-NXT1 traverses FG-repeat nucleoporins.
- Dbp5 (activated by Gle1 + inositol phosphate) remodels mRNP at cytoplasmic pore face.

## Results
Key consensus RNA elements:
- Yeast splice sites: 5′ss /GUAUGU, BP UACUAAC; vertebrate: 5′ss /GURAG, BP YURAY
- CPS (vertebrate polyadenylation signal): AAUAAA; cleavage site: CA
- >90% of human genes alternatively spliced; >50% have alternative 3′ ends
