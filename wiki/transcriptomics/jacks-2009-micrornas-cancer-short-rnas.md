## Summary
Ventura and Jacks (2009) review the evidence for miRNA involvement in cancer biology, cataloging oncogenic miRNAs (miR-155, miR-17~92 cluster) and tumor suppressor miRNAs (let-7, miR-34 family), describing the genetic and epigenetic alterations affecting miRNA genes in human tumors, and synthesizing data from genetically engineered mouse models. The paper establishes a framework for how miRNAs can function as oncogenes (when overexpressed, repressing tumor suppressors) or as tumor suppressors (when deleted or silenced, releasing oncogene expression).

## Key Contributions
- Systematic classification of tumor-promoting mechanisms: miRNA deletion/methylation (derepresses oncogenes); miRNA amplification/overexpression (represses tumor suppressors); 3'UTR mutations in oncogenes (escape miRNA repression; e.g., HMGA-2 translocation)
- Founding discovery: miR-15a/16-1 deletion at 13q14 in CLL (Calin/Croce 2002); targets BCL2 anti-apoptotic gene
- miR-155 as oncomiR: maps to BIC locus (originally identified as proviral insertion site in avian lymphomas); upregulated in hematopoietic malignancies, breast, lung, pancreas; ectopic expression in bone marrow → pre-B cell leukemia (Costinean et al., 2006); required for normal B and T cell function; regulates AID (activation-induced cytidine deaminase)
- miR-17~92 cluster: 6 miRNAs from single transcript at 13q31 amplicon; cooperates with c-Myc in B cell lymphomagenesis; transgenic overexpression → lymphoproliferative disorder; knockout → premature B cell death
- let-7 family: targets RAS; downregulated in lung cancer; inverse correlation with RAS expression
- miR-34 family: direct p53 transcriptional targets; pro-apoptotic; frequently epigenetically silenced in cancer; targets CDK4, CDK6, E2F3
- AID regulation by miR-155: mutation of single miR-155 binding site in AID 3'UTR partially phenocopies miR-155 knockout (increased AID → impaired affinity maturation)

## Methodology and Architecture
- Essay synthesizing human cancer genomics, cell line functional studies, and genetically engineered mouse models (GEMMs)
- miRNA mechanism: seed sequence (nt 2–8) pairs with seed-match in target 3'UTR; inhibits translation and reduces mRNA stability
- Table of mutation types: deletion, epigenetic silencing, amplification/translocation, point mutations affecting processing or target binding, 3'UTR rearrangements
- GEMMs key for causal validation: retroviral bone marrow transduction; transgenic overexpression; conditional/germline knockouts

## Results
- miR-155 gain-of-function: polyclonal pre-B cell proliferation followed by frank B cell leukemia OR myeloproliferation (depending on expression system)
- miR-155 loss-of-function: defective germinal center B cell reaction; impaired isotype-switched antibody production; role for AID regulation
- miR-17~92 gain-of-function: transgenic overexpression → lymphoproliferative disorder affecting B and T cells, eventual autoimmunity
- miR-17~92 loss-of-function: homozygous deletion → premature B cell death at pro-B/pre-B stage, lymphopenia; candidate mediator: Bim (pro-apoptotic BCL2 family member)
- miR-34 circuit: miR-34a/b/c transcription activated by p53 binding; connects miRNA pathway to p53 tumor suppressor network; loss of miR-34 function bypasses p53-mediated apoptosis
