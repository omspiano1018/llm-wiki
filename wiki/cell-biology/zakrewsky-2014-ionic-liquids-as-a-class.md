## Summary
Supporting Information for the original CAGE discovery paper (PNAS 2014). Provides complete synthesis and purification protocols for all 12 ILs/DES tested in the study. Most critically: geranic acid (technical grade, Sigma-Aldrich) must be purified by **repeated recrystallisation (5–7×) from 70 wt% geranic acid / 30 wt% acetone at −70°C** before use — necessary because commercial geranic acid is a racemic mixture. Choline geranate (compound 11) emerged as the lead material combining near-zero toxicity (IC₅₀ >> 2 mM), strong biofilm activity, and excellent skin penetration enhancement.

## Key Contributions
- **Geranic acid recrystallisation protocol**: 5–7× from 70% geranic acid / 30% acetone at −70°C — only synthesis protocol for purifying racemic commercial geranic acid in this paper set
- **Full IL panel**: 12 materials systematically compared; CAGE (11) selected as optimal
- **FTIR evidence**: ILs act as SC lipid **extractors**, not fluidizers
- **IC₅₀ table**: CAGE >> 2 mM (lowest toxicity of all tested ILs)
- **Physicochemical database**: viscosity, density, conductivity, Log P, MW for all 12 compounds

## Methodology and Architecture

### Geranic Acid Purification
```
Commercial geranic acid (technical grade, Sigma-Aldrich)
    ↓
Dissolve in 70 wt% geranic acid / 30 wt% acetone
    ↓
Recrystallise at −70°C  ×  5–7 times
    ↓
Check purity: 1H NMR + conductivity
```

### 12-IL Panel Rationale
Geraniol (parent alcohol of geranic acid) had the highest CPE enhancement ratio (47.5) and irritation potential rank among all chemical penetration enhancers — this drove design of CAGE (11) and compound 12.

## Results

### Toxicity (NHBE cells, IC₅₀)
- **CAGE (11): >> 2 mM** — effectively non-toxic
- Best safety profile of all 12 ILs tested
- Contrast: BZBN 0.01 mM, choline oleate 0.03 mM

### Skin Penetration
- CAGE enhanced delivery of both mannitol and cefadroxil into deep skin layers (SC, epidermis, dermis)
- Mechanism: lipid extraction from stratum corneum (FTIR-confirmed)

### Biofilm Activity
- CAGE active against S. enterica and P. aeruginosa 24-h biofilms
- Among most effective compounds with the best toxicity profile
