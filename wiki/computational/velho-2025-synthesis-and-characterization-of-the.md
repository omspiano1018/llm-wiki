## Summary
Velho et al. (2025) synthesize and fully characterize three **choline–amino acid ionic liquids (CAAILs)** — cholinium alaninate **[Ch][Ala]**, dicholinium glutamate **[Ch]₂[Glut]**, and cholinium threoninate **[Ch][Threo]** — and their aqueous binary solutions. These biobased, biodegradable, low-toxicity ILs are made by neutralizing each L-amino acid with KOH and then adding choline chloride. The paper reports a complete thermophysical dataset (density, refractive index, electrical conductivity, dynamic viscosity, torque, shear stress) over 288.15–308.15 K and determines each IL's **ionicity (degree of dissociation, α)** via the **Walden plot** — a property previously underexplored for CAAILs. It is a reference-data / property-characterization study rather than a biological one, providing the physicochemical groundwork for using these solvents in biomolecule extraction and stabilization.

## Key Contributions
- Synthesis and ¹H NMR/FTIR validation of **[Ch][Ala], [Ch]₂[Glut], [Ch][Threo]** (purities >97–99%).
- **Complete aqueous binary-solution characterization**: density, refractive index, conductivity, viscosity, torque, shear stress.
- **Ionicity (α)** vs composition by Walden plot — fills a recognized data gap for CAAILs.
- Robust model correlations: Connors–Wright (density), polynomial + Redlich–Kister (excess volume), VFTH/Arrhenius (viscosity).
- Reference thermodynamic data enabling rational design of CAAIL-based green solvents for pharmaceutics/bioengineering.

## Methodology and Architecture
**Synthesis** (313.15 K, 0.1 MPa): ethanolic L-amino acid + slight excess KOH (3 h) → add choline chloride (~12 h) → vacuum-filter KCl precipitate → rotary-evaporate dry (72 h, 323.15 K).
- [Ch][Ala] (C₈H₂₀N₂O₃), [Ch][Threo] (C₉H₂₂N₂O₄), [Ch]₂[Glut] (C₁₅H₃₅N₃O₆, dicationic).

**Measurements** on 9 binary solutions/system (Karl Fischer-corrected for water):
- Density (oscillating U-tube) at 288.15–308.15 K → **Connors–Wright** model.
- Excess molar volume Vₘᴱ → third-degree polynomials + **Redlich–Kister**.
- Viscosity (cone–plate rheometer): pure → **VFTH**, solutions → **Arrhenius**.
- Conductivity + viscosity → **Walden plot** for ionicity at 298.15 K.
- [Ch][Ala]: only refractive index newly measured (rest from prior group work).

## Results
| Property | Model | Fit quality (σ_SD) |
|----------|-------|--------------------|
| Liquid density ρ | Connors–Wright | 0.37 – 1.40 kg·m⁻³ |
| Excess volume Vₘᴱ | 3rd-deg polynomial | 0.0447 – 0.0729 (×10⁶ m³·mol⁻¹) |
| Excess volume Vₘᴱ | Redlich–Kister | 0.040 – 0.082 (×10⁶ m³·mol⁻¹) |
| Dynamic viscosity | Arrhenius | 1.14 – 2.32 (×10²) |
| Ionicity α | Walden plot | reported vs composition (298.15 K) |

Property trends are dominated by the amino-acid anion identity and by the dicationic stoichiometry of [Ch]₂[Glut].
