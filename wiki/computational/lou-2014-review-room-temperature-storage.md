## Summary
This 2014 UCLA review by Lou et al. evaluates room-temperature biospecimen storage modalities for anatomic pathology and biorepositories, comparing FFPE tissue, dry chemical/polymer matrices (FTA cards, Biomatrica), stabilization solutions (QIAGEN AllProtect), lyophilization, and formalin-free fixatives. The primary motivation is reducing costs and risks of frozen (−80°C) storage while maintaining nucleic acid and protein quality for downstream molecular analyses.

## Key Contributions
- FTA cards: best-studied matrix for DNA; forensic studies show utility after >7 years; manufacturer does not support RNA storage
- Biomatrica chemical matrices: DNAstable shows ~3.66 years real-time stability; vendor claims 60-year DNA and 12-year RNA stability by accelerated aging
- Commercial RNA matrices (RNAstable, GenTegra): 336 RNA samples stored 2 weeks at RT = −80°C for RNA quality and RT-PCR
- QIAGEN AllProtect: preserves DNA, RNA, protein in tissue up to 7 days at RT and DNA for 6 months
- Lyophilization: preserves histomorphology, DNA, RNA, protein but RNA degrades moderately after 1-2 years
- No existing RT technology simultaneously preserves tissue DNA, RNA, and protein long-term (years)

## Methodology and Architecture
Review of PubMed and vendor literature evaluating four storage modalities:
1. **Dry chemical/polymer matrices**: desiccation in proprietary mixtures; exploit anhydrobiosis principle; DNA-only or RNA-only preservation
2. **Stabilization solutions**: liquid reagents permeating cells and inactivating nucleases; short-term tissue preservation
3. **Lyophilization**: sublimation of water under vacuum; solid-state preservation
4. **Formalin-free fixatives**: improved nucleic acid preservation over FFPE but face validation and cost barriers

FFPE baseline: cross-linked and fragmented nucleic acids, ~1 mutation per 500 bases, loss of enzymatic activity; poor RNA quality for many assays.

## Results
- FTA paper (Flinders Technology Associates): cellulose with uric acid + detergent + chelating agent; cells lysed on contact; DNA preserved; no protein or RNA long-term
- Biomatrica DNAstable/SampleMatrix: demonstrated equivalence to −20°C storage by qPCR, gel electrophoresis, multiplex STR analysis
- Biomatrica/IntegenX GenTegra RNA: 2-week RT storage equivalent to −80°C for RNA quality and RT-PCR performance
- AllProtect: protects all three biomolecule classes short-term; key for buffer against freezer failure
- Independent validation data (especially for long-term storage, whole exome/genome sequencing) largely absent
