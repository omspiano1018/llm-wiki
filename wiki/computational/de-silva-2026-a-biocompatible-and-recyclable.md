## Summary
De Silva et al. (2026) introduce the first ionic liquid (IL) platform designed for stabilizing heterogeneous total plant RNA. Among choline-based ILs tested, **20% (w/v) choline glutamate** emerged as the optimal medium, protecting RNA from thermal degradation up to 80–90°C, maintaining integrity for ≥72 h at 40°C and several weeks at room temperature, and allowing the IL to be recycled for ≥5 cycles without degrading RNA quality. The work closes a gap in the field: prior IL-based RNA stabilization efforts focused on purified or short-chain commercial RNAs, not the complex, heterogeneous RNA systems relevant to plant biology and field sampling.

## Key Contributions
- **Choline glutamate (20% w/v)** identified as optimal stabilizer for total plant RNA
- **Thermal protection**: RNA integrity retained at 80–90°C; controls degraded at 70°C
- **Long-term stability**: ≥72 h at 40°C; delayed degradation by weeks at ambient temperature
- **5-cycle recyclability**: IL recovered and reused without loss of RNA purity or integrity
- **Multi-metric validation**: NanoDrop (A260/280, A260/230), Qubit fluorometry, and Bioanalyzer electrophoresis used in combination — a more rigorous framework than gel-only approaches

## Methodology and Architecture
Total plant RNA was extracted from plant tissue and incubated in different choline-based IL solutions. RNA quality was assessed across a temperature range (room temperature to 90°C) and time course (hours to weeks). The IL environment inactivates ribonucleases via the non-hydrolytic milieu created by the choline cation. After use, RNA is recovered and the IL is recycled by standard precipitation/filtration protocols.

|-----------|------------------|---------------|
| Degradation onset | 80–90°C | 70°C |
| 40°C stability | ≥72 h | Faster degradation |
| Room temp stability | Delayed by weeks | Baseline |
| Recycling | ≥5 cycles | N/A |

## Results
- Choline glutamate outperformed other choline-based ILs (choline hydroxide, other organic acid combinations) in thermal protection and long-term stability
- RNA purity metrics (A260/280, A260/230) and fluorescence-based quantification remained within acceptable ranges after IL treatment and recovery
- Closed-loop recyclability demonstrated: no measurable RNA integrity loss across 5 reuse cycles
- No requirement for dry ice or ultra-low temperature storage during transport
