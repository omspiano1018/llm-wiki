## Summary
A delivery-focused 2024 review on ionic liquids as **carriers for nucleic-acid therapeutics** (DNA, siRNA, mRNA, miRNA, ASO). Where [[computational/egorova-2021-ionic-liquids-prospects-for-nucleic]] maps the whole IL–nucleic-acid field, Mirhadi narrows in on the **delivery / nano-vector** angle: ILs as biocompatible solubilizers and stabilizers of nucleic acids, as cellular-uptake enhancers, and as components of liposome/nanoparticle vectors that raise transfection efficiency. Good second read for someone building RNA delivery, especially for the nano-formulation framing.

## Key Contributions
- Dedicated to nucleic-acid **therapeutics** and their delivery barriers (large MW, strong negative charge, nuclease sensitivity, poor membrane permeation)
- Two IL roles for delivery: **direct solubilizer/stabilizer + uptake enhancer**, and **building block of nano-vectors** (liposomes, nanoparticles)
- Eco-friendly / green-chemistry framing; favors biocompatible choline- and amino-acid-derived ILs
- Positions IL-enabled nucleic-acid transport as a major emerging therapeutic modality

## Methodology and Architecture
Narrative review covering: IL fundamentals and tunability; IL solubilization mechanisms (hydrotropy, cosolvency, micellization); nucleic-acid classes and their secondary structures (duplex/triplex/G-quadruplex); IL strategies for extraction, concentration, stabilization, and intracellular delivery; and integration of ILs into nano delivery systems with transfection outcomes.

## Results
- IL-driven aqueous **solubility enhancement** demonstrated across many poorly soluble APIs (amphotericin B, itraconazole, 5-FU, paclitaxel, …) — evidence of solvation power transferable to formulation
- API-IL catalog (e.g., lidocainium ibuprofenate, procainium salicylate) retaining bioactivity
- IL formulations reported to **preserve native DNA/RNA structure**, resist nuclease degradation, and **enhance pDNA/siRNA transfection** via IL-containing liposomes and nanoparticles
- Headline: effective **RNA delivery to target sites** achieved with IL-based systems

## Design takeaways for RNA delivery
- Use **biocompatible choline/amino-acid ILs** as the safer starting chemistry.
- ILs can act either as the **formulation additive** (solubility/stability/uptake) or as a **structural component of the nano-vector** — decide which role fits your platform.
- Pairs naturally with established LNP/liposome work — see the concrete mRNA-LNP instances below.

## Limitations
- Toxicity / long-term safety the main barrier; biocompatible ILs are the favored route
- Sparse standardized in vivo efficacy & safety data; scalability and regulatory acceptance unresolved
- Field nascent — emphasis on potential rather than approved products
